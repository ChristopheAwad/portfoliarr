## Product contract and scope

1. Horizontal gestures on the allocation donut never switch its six views. The previous/next arrow buttons and six pagination dots are the only view-switch controls. Keep wraparound, the current/total counter, the active dot, saved view in `allocationDimension`, animation and reduced-motion behavior, and the existing portfolio-specific fetch and cache guards.
2. A finger can still touch a donut segment to inspect its tooltip. On finger lift or touch cancellation, clear the tooltip and active segment, and suppress delayed synthetic mouse/touch events that would reopen it. A finger drag must not trigger navigation. Preserve normal vertical page scrolling over the donut; do not apply a `touch-action: none` rule or change the line chart's separate touch interactions in `common.js`.
3. Show an honest empty state. An empty ledger, fully closed positions, or no positive long positions may say that no held allocation is available. If a non-ticker allocation response has zero slices but one or more `excluded` entries, explain that the held tickers could not be priced or classified for this view. Keep the existing per-ticker reasons in `#alloc-excluded`, and do not call this an empty portfolio. For the By Ticker view, use the summary response's `unpriced` list when no priced long slices are available; do not mislabel an all-quote/all-FX failure as no holdings. Do not invent a slice or alter the backend's CAD/long-only weighting rules.
4. If the By Ticker summary request fails after a successful paint, show that its allocation is from the last successful reply. If no successful By Ticker result exists, show the unavailable state instead of an empty donut. A later successful response clears the stale/error notice. Do not let a failed summary request for an old portfolio change the currently selected portfolio's donut. Other allocation dimensions keep their current cached-payload and revalidation behavior.
5. When the selected portfolio changes, immediately hide the prior donut, clear the old portfolio's excluded-ticker note, and wait for the new selection's own response. An old in-flight response must never paint across that switch. The active dimension may stay selected; its cached data must be keyed to the portfolio and must not show another portfolio's values.
